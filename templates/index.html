How does it work?

  • Provide your Network Details

    To defend your SDN, we need its details, our respective form will ask you to provide those details and you can enter them in a seemless manner.

  • Sit back & Let the magic happen

    Once the data is fed to the detection emgine, the magic of computing begins and we get to know a denial of service attack occured. If yes, our mitigation strategy follows up.

  • Mitigate & Stay safe

    If a DDoS is in potential, the suspected IP will be given back to you and you can easily block that specific IP and stay safe and in turn keep your systems up and running.

Frequently Asked Questions

Software-Defined Networking (SDN) is vulnerable to DDoS attacks due to its centralized control, making it a single point of failure. Attackers can overwhelm the controller with traffic, disrupting network management and traffic flows, potentially causing service outages.

DefendSDN employs the XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) algorithm for its DDoS detection and mitigation tasks. XGBoost is renowned for its high accuracy, speed, and adaptability, making it a powerful tool for safeguarding SDN environments against cyber threats.
